UC is the positive class. Delta AUC is ratio minus comparator. Intervals are pointwise 95% DeLong intervals. Two-sided paired tests use retrospective Holm correction across four comparisons within each cohort. Development comparisons are exploratory and do not account for score selection; summed IgG is a post hoc addition. HMP2 is context only. Nonsignificance does not establish equivalence. Expanded multiplicity sensitivity is in Table S4.

The score is log2[(G + 0.5)/(A + M + 0.5)], where G = IGHG1 + IGHG2 + IGHG3 + IGHG4, A = IGHA1 + IGHA2, and M = IGHM. Total Ig additionally includes IGHD, IGKC, IGLC1, IGLC2, and IGLC3. IGHG3-to-other-Ig is log2[(IGHG3 + 0.5)/(total Ig - IGHG3 + 0.5)]. Abundance transforms are cohort-specific and remain defined in the analysis modules.
